Products I Use

Every product I bring into your rental is on the record.

I don’t buy what’s on the shelf. I look up what’s in it first.

Before a product comes into your vacation rental, I go through the label ingredient by ingredient. This is the database I use. It’s how I know what cuts hard water without dulling your floors, what I keep away from kids and pets, and what stays off well water and septic systems. Not everything labeled green is gentle. Not everything that cleans well belongs under your sink. I check both before I use it.

You can look it up too. Search by product, by brand, or by the ingredient you’d rather not have in your home. If you see something I use and you want to know why, ask me. I’d rather you ask than wonder.
